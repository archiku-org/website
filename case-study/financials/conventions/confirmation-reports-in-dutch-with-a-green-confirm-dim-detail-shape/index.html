Confirmation reports in Dutch with a green-confirm dim-detail shape

Title

Confirmation reports in Dutch with a green-confirm dim-detail shape

Convention Statement

The confirmation surfaces speak Dutch, lead with a green "Bevestigd:…" receipt and follow with dim-bracketed detail lines (balances, register note, skip notes) — one visual pattern across every commit path.

Summary

A repo-internal reporting convention: green confirmation receipts with dim follow-up details, warnings in yellow, refusals in red — identical across the plan, instance and group paths.

Description

All confirm paths print their success as a green "Bevestigd: {old} → {new}" line, immediately followed by a dim line carrying the checking and spaar balances (and, on the plan path, the "expected entry verwijderd" note). Skip reasons inside group walks are dim or yellow one-liners naming the member id and the reason; refusals are red. Future-date warnings and the transfer caution are yellow. This shape is repo-internal presentation glue, not business behavior — the colors and the Dutch wording are consistent enough to treat as a convention a reimplementation should mirror for a familiar look.
