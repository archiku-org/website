    financials

    Scope. 75 modules, 26 components, 148 business-rules, 37 concepts, 7 processes, 9 non-goals, 11 surface commands — the analysis base this summary condenses (business-rules: deterministic sample of 50 of 148; all other payload families read in full).

    Archetype. An append-only journal-first finance CLI with a terminal UI: committed money lives only in a content-hash-integrity journal folded into a date-positioned ledger; forecasts (expected register, recurrence rules, groups) are projections that never themselves move money.

    Top capabilities. Three-mode quick add with balance-confirmation gate; one-step confirm of landed open rows (single retires-and-lands mutation); guided edit/delete across the full typed id space (t####/e####/g####/r:); virtual recurrence rules with human-gated detection; group rollups in the view only; a composed projection serving list, TUI and Rich reports with Overdracht-excluded flows.

    Non-goals that shape it. No reconcile/account-creation ops — accounts emerge from postings and unexplained money is an explicit reviewed correction; rules never materialize; groups never journal; reporting never reconciles.

    Start here. Read the product summary for capability-by-capability detail, then the archetype mapping for how the concepts live in the components; use financials list as the designated id source for anything edit/confirm/delete.

    Verification status. 4 objections are recorded in the vault by the verification phase and remain open — none are resolved in this engagement; read them before treating any claim above as final.
