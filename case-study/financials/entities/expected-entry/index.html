    Expected entry (model.Transaction in the expected store)

    Name

    Expected entry

    Title

    Expected entry (model.Transaction in the expected store)

    Summary

    A future-dated promised movement held in its own store with status pending, carrying no balances until it lands as an actual transaction.

    Description

    The promise side of the books: a rent due Friday, a salary expected next week. It shares the cashflow row's shape — date, description, category, amount — but its status marks it as expected, its balances are empty by design, and it keeps both the raw and the canonical form of its category. Each promised movement gets an identifier from its register order. When the day arrives and the movement really happens, confirming retires this entry and the realized movement enters the journaled books.

    State Management

    Status marks the lifecycle: expected while the promise is open, actual once confirmed — the confirmed form becoming a journaled ledger row rather than a changed field on this record. The store is a plain list saved whole; no chain, no hashes: a promise has no balances to protect.
