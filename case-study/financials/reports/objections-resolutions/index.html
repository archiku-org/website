    Objections & Resolutions

    Note

    When a review re-reads an analysis fact against the code and the evidence disagrees, the reviewer raises an objection: it names the contested fact and lays out which piece of evidence fails. Every objection goes to the arbiter, whose ruling is captured as a resolution — upheld (the fact gets corrected or retired) or rejected (the fact stands). A case stays open until a resolution names it. This report follows the cases; the full statement behind each case lives on the fact pages every heading links to.
