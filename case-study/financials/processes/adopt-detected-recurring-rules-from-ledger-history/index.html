    Adopt detected recurring rules from ledger history

    Process Name

    Recurrence detection and adoption

    Title

    Adopt detected recurring rules from ledger history

    Summary

    Mines the real payment history for repeating patterns, shows them as a review-only proposal list, and turns a proposal into a standing schedule only when the owner walks the adoption form to its end.

    Description

    The same bill keeps arriving and retyping it is waste. The owner asks the books which payments look like habits, and the books answer with a list built from money that actually moved: payments of the same name and category, grouped, their rhythm judged from the spacing of their dates, their amount taken from the middle of what was really paid. Payments that already sit under a standing rule pause silently — the list never grows back.

    There are two tiers of answer. The steady ones — subscriptions, rent — propose their middle value with nothing more said. The wanderers — budget-style payments that arrive on time for changing figures — propose the same middle value but carry their observed low and high openly, so the owner sees the spread at a glance. A payment that swings between income and expense is refused as one habit in either tier.

    The list itself is inert: nothing anywhere is created by looking. Adopting means naming a number, and the books open a form with that payment's values already filled in — the owner accepts each field with Enter or replaces it by typing, and may quit at any point, which stores nothing. Only the completed walk passes the admission checks (approved category, non-zero amount, sane dates), and only then does the rule enter the books; its next three anticipated dates are shown straight after, so the owner sees the schedule sitting in the forward calendar. From that point the forecast generates its instances without further typing.

    Trigger

    Repeating payments have accumulated in the ledger history and the owner acts on them — surface: the recurrence detect and recurrence take commands

    BPMN XML

    process "Owner"
  start "Spots a repeating payment"
  user "Ask which payments repeat"
  receive "Read the proposal list"
  user "Name a proposal to adopt"
  exclusive "Adoption completed or abandoned?"
    if "Nothing was saved" then
      receive "Read that nothing was stored"
    else "The rule was saved"
      receive "Read the rule's next dates"
  end "Repeating payment decided"

process "Recurrence planner"
  start "History question arrives"
  rule "Re-derive proposal candidates from the ledger history"
  exclusive "Review list or numbered adoption?"
    if "Owner wants the review list" then
      send "Present the review-only proposal list"
    else "Owner adopts a numbered proposal"
      exclusive "Proposal number still on the list?"
        if "Gone" then
          send "Report that the proposal is no longer there"
          end "Adoption never opened"
        else "On the list"
    user "Seed every field with the detected value"
    exclusive "Quit key pressed at a field?"
      if "Quit" then
        send "Report that nothing was stored"
        end "Adoption abandoned"
      else "Every field completed"
    rule "Check the completed rule against the admission bars"
    exclusive "Rule admissible?"
      if "Refused" then
        send "Report the refusal with its reason"
        end "Adoption refused"
      else "Admissible"
    task "Save the rule into the standing schedules"
    send "Show the rule's next three anticipated dates"
  end "Run done"
