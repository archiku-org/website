    Confirm a landed open entry into the books

    Process Name

    Open entry confirmation

    Title

    Confirm a landed open entry into the books

    Summary

    Turns a landed expected entry or rule occurrence into a real ledger posting, closes the open item behind it, and reports the new account state — refusing any item whose money has not actually moved.

    Description

    Money has moved at (or before) the day it was promised — the moment every forecast lives for — and the owner brings the movement into the books. The item is named by address, or picked from the desk's open list when nothing is named; a no-op answer follows when the books already record the address. A landed item commits exactly as it was promised, without a single question: the posting enters as a real journal entry with running balances on both accounts, and the promise behind it is retired — its old address carried on the booking's own record, so no open entry lingers. When the item is a whole group, every landed member commits in day order, each member's address is re-pointed to its new committed one, and the group keeps its still-future members. A booking that drifted from its standing rule (different amount, or too far from the planned day) records the occurrence as an exception; a booking that matches supersedes the occurrence on its own. Money that has not moved yet is never confirmed — a future item is refused and left open, an item without an amount is refused outright, and a group quietly skips such members with a note. A transfer confirmation warns that both accounts moved together. The outcome returns to the owner with the new account state.

    Trigger

    An expected or projected movement has landed in the account and the owner accepts it into the books — surface: the confirm command (bare, e-number, rule instance or group id)

    Note

    Steps live in src/financials/confirm.py: dispatch confirm_transaction 232-261, the picker _pick_open 205-229, the single-row desk _confirm_expected 64-94 and its commit core _commit_expected 38-61, the instance desk _confirm_instance 97-121, the group desk _confirm_group 124-202. Posting construction and the idempotent apply live in commands.py (cmd_add 98-125, postings_for 88-95) and journal.py (ConfirmMutation.apply_on 271-284; Ledger.insert 206-218, propagate 220-225). The open-list backlog is computed in ledger_view.py (open_rows 235-242, _projection_walk 161-202). BPMN validate: valid on first-composed document after one end-event-routing repair cycle.

    process "Owner"
  start "Landed money waits to be accepted"
  user "Decide to bring the open item into the books"
  send "Name the open item to confirm"
  receive "Take the desk's answer"
  end "The landed movement is settled in the books"

process "Open-entry confirmation"
  start "Confirm request arrives"
  exclusive "Is an open item named?"
    if "No" then
      service "Compose the landed open list"
      user "Pick an open item or cancel"
      exclusive "Was an item picked?"
        if "No" then
          end "Nothing confirmed" message
        else "Picked"
    else "An item is named"
  service "Resolve the open item from register or standing rule"
  exclusive "Does the item still lie ahead of today?"
    if "Yes" then
      send "Refuse to confirm money that has not moved"
      end "The open item stays open" message
    else "Landed"
  exclusive "Does the item carry its amount?"
    if "No" then
      send "Refuse to confirm a movement with no amount"
      end "The item without amount stays open" message
    else "Amount stated"
  exclusive "Is the open item a group?"
    if "Yes" then
      service "Collect the group's landed members in day order"
      exclusive "Did any member land?"
        if "Yes" then
          service "Commit the next landed member as an actual posting"
          service "Retire the member's open item and re-point its address"
          exclusive "More landed members pending?"
            if "Yes" goto "Commit the next landed member as an actual posting"
            else "No" goto "Report the outcome with the new account state"
        else "No" then
          end "The group stands as it was" message
    else "A single item"
  service "Commit the item as an actual journal posting"
  service "Retire the open item behind the posting"
  exclusive "Did the posting drift from its standing rule?"
    if "Yes" then
      service "Record the occurrence as an exception"
    else "On mark"
  send "Report the outcome with the new account state"
  end "Open item committed" message
