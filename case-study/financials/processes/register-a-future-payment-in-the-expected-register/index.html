    Register a future payment in the expected register

    Process Name

    Expected entry intake

    Title

    Register a future payment in the expected register

    Summary

    An upcoming payment or income is stated ahead of its day and the books park it as an open promise — invisible to actual balances, visible to the forecast — until confirmation turns it into a real movement.

    Description

    A bill or a payment the owner already knows will come — a rent due next week, a salary on its way, a tax bill — should be visible in the forecast before the money actually moves. The owner states the amount, the day, and what it is for. There are two doors for this. The expectation door is where a fully stated future movement goes on purpose. The ordinary statement door is just as good: whatever the owner states in pieces gets completed first, and any completed statement dated after today is shown the way to the expectation door — the books refuse to record a future movement as if it had happened. The expectation door checks the statement completely: the day must be a real calendar day, the description must say what is coming, the category must be approved, an amount must be stated. Each shortcoming is refused with a plainly worded reason, nothing is stored. A promise must also lie ahead of today — the actual ledger enforces the same border from its side, so money that has already moved can never end up as a promise. On approval the promise gets its own identity and is parked: it weighs nothing against real balances, shows up as an open item in every view, and awaits its day. The owner can rehearse the registration first — a rehearsal shows exactly what would be stored and stores nothing. Confirmation, once the day has come, turns the promise into a real ledger entry and the cycle closes there.

    Trigger

    An upcoming payment or income became known and lies ahead of today — surface: the expect command, or the add command when the entered date is in the future

    BPMN XML

    process "Owner"
  start "An upcoming movement becomes known"
  exclusive "How is it stated?"
    if "Piecewise or quick-shorthand" then
      user "State the movement ahead of today"
    else "The expect door, stated in full" user "Register the expectation directly"
  receive "Read the outcome"
  end "Expectation held visibly in the forecast"

process "Entry intake"
  start "Statement arrives"
  exclusive "Stated for a day ahead?"
    if "Yes" then
      send "Hand the promise to the register"
      end "Statement handed onward"
    else "Already today or past" then
      end "Left to the actual ledger"

process "Expected register"
  start "Promise arrives"
  rule "Check the promise statement"
  exclusive "Does the statement hold?"
    if "No" then
      send "Refuse with the reason"
      end "Promise refused"
    else "Yes"
  service "Assign the register id"
  exclusive "Was this a rehearsal?"
    if "Rehearsal, store nothing" then
      send "Preview what would be stored"
      end "Nothing stored"
    else "Real registration"
  service "Park the promise in the register"
  end "Promise parked" message

message flows
  from "Owner"."State the movement ahead of today" to "Entry intake"."Statement arrives" label "Movement statement"
  from "Owner"."Register the expectation directly" to "Expected register"."Promise arrives" label "Expectation statement"
  from "Entry intake"."Hand the promise to the register" to "Expected register"."Promise arrives" label "Promise request"
  from "Expected register"."Refuse with the reason" to "Owner"."Read the outcome" label "Refusal with reason"
  from "Expected register"."Preview what would be stored" to "Owner"."Read the outcome" label "Rehearsal preview"
  from "Expected register"."Promise parked" to "Owner"."Read the outcome" label "Parked with its id"
