Group statement rows

Process Name

Group statement rows

Title

Group statement rows

Summary

A card statement's charges are bundled into a named group with a rollup date; the ledger view shows one folder-prefixed rollup line with the summed amount landing on the payment day, confirming commits the landed charges into the ledger without ever modifying their rows, and deleting the group un-bundles while every charge survives.

Description

The owner receives a card statement whose individual charges are already rows in the registers. They name the bundling — a group with a label, its own rollup date (the payment day), an optional category and note — and add the charge ids to it in one all-or-nothing action: an add naming an unknown id changes nothing.

From then on the ledger view shows the group as one virtual rollup line: "📁 Mastercard (12)" with the member sum, dated to the payment day (or the charges' latest day when none was set). The projection walks the collapsed row set, so no charge touches the balance before the rollup date and the total lands on it.

When the statement total is collected, the owner confirms the group: every charge that has actually landed commits in date order — reserved rows become committed entries with the membership list re-pointed to the new ids — while future, dangling, already-committed and already-covered charges are skipped with a note. A group confirm never destroys the group; a hand-booking that already covers a charge (same category, matching amount, near date) prevents a double post.

In the history view a fully settled statement shows as the one rollup line with the last landed charge's balance; a partially settled one shows the settled charges individually while the rest still rolls up in the open section. The owner dissolves the bundling with a typed confirmation whenever they want — every charge remains its own row. Groups are organizational metadata, not money: they never enter the money journal, and no group action ever modifies a member row.

Trigger

A card statement arrives whose charges need one combined view and rollup. Surface: the financials group command (create, add, remove, edit, show, delete, list).

BPMN XML

process "Owner"
  start "A card statement has arrived and its charges need one combined view"
  task "Name the statement's charges and set their rollup date"
  task "Add the charge ids to the bundling, all-or-nothing"
  task "See the statement as one rollup line whose total lands on the payment day"
  exclusive "Have the charges landed on the account yet?"
    if "Not yet" then
      task "Wait for the payment day to arrive"
    else "Yes, some already booked by hand"
      task "Note which charges a hand-booking already covers"
  task "Confirm the landed charges into the ledger in date order"
  task "See the statement as one settled line in the history view"
  exclusive "Is the statement bundling still worth keeping?"
    if "No" then
      task "Un-group the bundling, leaving every charge as its own row"
    else "Yes" then
      task "Keep editing the bundling's label, rollup date or note"
  end "The statement is bundled exactly as it should be"
