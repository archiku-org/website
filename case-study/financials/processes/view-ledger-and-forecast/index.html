View ledger and forecast

Process Name

View ledger and forecast

Title

View ledger and forecast

Summary

The owner reviews the household's money position through three windows on one composed picture — the printed ledger list, the interactive scrollable view with detail and add dialogs, or the monthly report — always seeing the committed history, what has landed but is not booked yet, and the forecast ahead.

Description

Whenever the owner looks at their money, the program assembles one picture and shows it through the window they asked for. The picture joins three layers: the committed history from the event journal, the open section of movements that have landed but were never confirmed, and the projection ahead built only from inputs the owner explicitly declared — approved recurring rules and recorded one-off expectations. Where a real entry has landed for a period a rule also predicts, the prediction steps aside; what landed but is unconfirmed stays in view until confirmation, so nothing silently disappears on the way to being booked.

The printed list shows the recent booked days, the open entries, the coming days' projection and a footer that always answers the longer question: the projected balance at month end and year end, and the moments where an account is predicted to dip below zero. Narrowing the view by a search term hides rows for the eye only — balances and positions never move for a filter.

The interactive view scrolls the same picture with the full history back to the beginning, the projection always running to year end. The owner can open a row's detail (booked, open or predicted is stated) or add a new movement; a completed add re-lands the view with the cursor on the new row, while a cancelled one leaves everything untouched. Reading has no side effects anywhere — the picture changes only through the owner's own add, edit or confirm actions elsewhere.

Fully committed groups collapse into single total rows so a card statement reads as one line; a group with anything unconfirmed keeps showing its members individually. The monthly report is the retrospective window: strictly booked entries, per-month income, expense and net with end-of-month balances, the top categories, and the balance curve — never mixing in expectations, and never counting a transfer between the owner's own accounts as income or expense.

Trigger

The owner wants to see where the money stands — committed history, what is still open, and what the coming period brings. Surface: the financials list command, the financials tui command, and the financials report command.

BPMN XML

process "Owner"
  start "The owner wants to see where the money stands"
  exclusive "Which window on the money?"
    if "A quick printed snapshot" then
      send "Ask for the printed ledger"
      receive "Read the printed snapshot"
    if "An interactive browse" then
      send "Open the interactive ledger"
      receive "Use the interactive view"
    else "A monthly retrospective" then
      send "Ask for the monthly report"
      receive "Read the retrospective"
  end "The picture is taken in"

process "Ledger composition"
  start "Receive a view's request"
  service "Boot the committed ledger from the journal"
  service "Expand the approved recurring rules over the window"
  service "Drop predictions a real entry already covers"
  service "Split what landed but is unconfirmed from what is still ahead"
  service "Walk the running balance through every row"
  service "Collapse fully committed groups into one total row"
  service "Warn where an account is predicted to dip below zero"
  end "The money picture is composed"

process "Ledger list window"
  start "Receive the composed picture for printing"
  task "Print the booked rows, the open entries and the projection"
  end "Snapshot printed"

process "Interactive ledger"
  start "Receive the composed picture for the view"
  task "Show the history, the open entries and the projection to year end"
  exclusive "What does the owner do next?"
    if "Open a row's details"
      task "Read the row's full detail dialog"
      goto "What does the owner do next?"
    if "Add a new movement"
      task "Fill in the add dialog and save"
      task "Re-land the view on the newly added row"
      goto "What does the owner do next?"
    else "Done looking"
  end "Browse closed"

process "Monthly report"
  start "Receive the composed picture for the report"
  task "Select only the booked entries in scope"
  task "Sum the monthly flows and the category totals"
  task "Draw the balance curve across months"
  end "Report printed"
