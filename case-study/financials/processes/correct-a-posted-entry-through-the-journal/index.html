    Correct a posted entry through the journal

    Process Name

    Entry correction

    Title

    Correct a posted entry through the journal

    Summary

    Fixes date, description, category or amount of an entry after posting, by appending corrective mutations instead of rewriting history.

    Description

    A posted entry turns out wrong — a typo in the amount, the wrong category, a date that belongs to another month. The owner picks the entry by its address, or from the last ten rows when no address springs to mind. The desk resolves the address first: the address may name a committed row, a promise for the future, one occurrence of a standing rule, or a whole group — and the same correction walk is not even used for the last two, the books hand over to the matching editor instead. An address matching nothing ends with an unknown-address refusal and nothing has changed. On a committed row the fields — day, description, category, amount — appear one at a time, each filled with the current value. Enter keeps the value; three failed answers on a field abandon the whole correction, again with nothing changed. Correcting a row to a day after today does not keep it in the books: the row is backed out and its corrected content is reserved as a promise for its day, and the owner is told plainly when that reservation is refused. A correction that keeps the entry in the books appends an update record; the balance chain re-applies from that record so every later row reflects the fix, and the owner sees the new checking balance. The promises register has its own two outcomes. A promise whose day has arrived commits into the books as the restated content — one retiring bookkeeping record, then the open entry leaves the register. A promise still in the future is simply restated where it stands. Transfers in either desk warn that the savings side moved too. Every committed repair goes through one write step — the record is stamped, appended to the journal and the books re-folded — so a correction never rewrites history and stays auditable later.

    Trigger

    A posted or expected entry shows a mistake and the owner revises it — surface: the edit command (id, or pick from the last ten rows)

    Note

    Steps live in edit.py (resolver 274-319, walk 66-113, desks 165-271), the write step in commands.py, the engine mechanics in journal.py; script composed and validated this pass.

    BPMN XML

    process "Owner"
  start "A posted entry shows a mistake"
  user "Pick the entry to correct"
  send "Correction request"
  receive "Correction outcome"
  end "Books corrected"

process "Entry editor"
  start "Correction requested"
  user "Review the entry field by field"
  exclusive "Which register holds the entry?"
    if "The books" then
      exclusive "Restated day after today?"
        if "Yes" then
          send "Back the row out of the books"
          send "Reserve the corrected row as a promise"
          exclusive "Reservation refused?"
            if "Yes" then
              task "Leave the recovery to the owner"
              end "Row left unreplaced"
            else "No" then
              task "Report the completed move"
              goto "Write accepted"
        else "No"
      send "Land the corrective record"
    if "The promises register" then
      exclusive "Day already arrived?"
        if "Yes" then
          send "Confirm the restated promise into the books"
          task "Retire the promise from the register"
          goto "Write accepted"
        else "No" then
          task "Restate the promise where it stands"
          goto "Write accepted"
    if "A group or a standing rule" then
      task "Open the group or occurrence editor"
      end "Corrected elsewhere"
    else "Unresolvable address" then
      task "Refuse the unknown address"
      end "Nothing changed"
  receive "Write accepted"
  user "Report the outcome"
  end "Correction finished"

process "Command mutations"
  start "Write requested"
  service "Append the corrective mutation"
  service "Refold the balance chain"
  end "Journal converged"

message flows
  from "Owner"."Correction request" to "Entry editor"."Correction requested" label "Edit request"
  from "Entry editor"."Back the row out of the books" to "Command mutations"."Write requested" label "Row back-out"
  from "Entry editor"."Reserve the corrected row as a promise" to "Command mutations"."Write requested" label "Promise reservation"
  from "Entry editor"."Land the corrective record" to "Command mutations"."Write requested" label "Committed correction"
  from "Entry editor"."Confirm the restated promise into the books" to "Command mutations"."Write requested" label "Landed promise commits"
  from "Command mutations"."Journal converged" to "Entry editor"."Write accepted" label "Write result"
  from "Entry editor"."Report the outcome" to "Owner"."Correction outcome" label "Correction result"
