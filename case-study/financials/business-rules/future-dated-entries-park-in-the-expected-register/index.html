Future-dated entries park in the expected register

Title

Future-dated entries park in the expected register

Rule Statement

A transaction dated after today is not recorded in the actual books — it is saved as a plan (an expected entry) in a separate store with fixed status "expected" and no balance carrying, regardless of which surface the bookkeeper used.

Summary

Any future-dated entry — typed via add flags, shorthand or the prompts — routes to the expected-register store instead of the journaled ledger.

Description

When a household movement is recorded with a date later than today, the entry does not enter the actual books: it is parked as a plan. The routing is applied uniformly at every path of the add surface — fully-flagged input, shorthand-seeded input, and prompted input all compare the date against today and divert to the expected register. The parked entry carries the same business fields (date, description, category, amount) but no balance projections: nothing has actually happened yet.
