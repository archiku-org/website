Only committed rows are deleted — plans are withdrawn, not deleted

Title

Only committed rows are deleted — plans are withdrawn, not deleted

Rule Statement

A future-dated entry is never deleted from history (it has none); removing it removes it silently from the register of expectations only, leaving the ledger untouched.

Summary

The safe variant of removal for things that never happened yet — the future row vanishes from the plan register, no history event is written, the ledger is untouched.

Description

A movement that has not happened yet is not history, so removing it must not look like history being destroyed. The register of expectations holds the future rows; removal takes the row out of that register directly. No removal event is appended, the ledger is unchanged, and no balances move — there is nothing committed to reverse. The owner still confirms first, but the act is a quiet correction of a plan, not a mutation of the books. This is the boundary between plans and facts again: the future can be edited or withdrawn freely, the past only through its audited discipline. Traced at _delete_expected (src/financials/delete.py:48-56: load_expected, filter the row out by id, save_expected — no journal involvement).
