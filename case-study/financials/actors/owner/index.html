The owner

Name

Owner

Title

The owner

Kind

role

Summary

The person whose money this is — they capture movements the moment they happen, by phrase, by form or by conversation, and they see every saved row with its new saldos.

Description

The owner is the one party this process exists for: they pay, they receive, they move money, and they want it recorded as it happens. In this process they state the movement — its date, description, category and amount — and everything they do not state is asked, never assumed.

Their stakes run through the whole flow. They are the one whose answer to the saldo question is trusted when it disagrees with the projection; they choose to abort when a capture feels wrong, and then nothing is stored; and they see the saved row with its updated account balances immediately after each save, closing the loop on every capture.

They also own the house's care rules — the approved categories and the refusal to guess — and the boundary between facts and plans is theirs to feel: a movement that has not happened is dated in the future and lands in the register of expectations, not the ledger.
