The headache

Somewhere in your organization runs software nobody fully understands anymore: its original developers are gone, its documentation is dead, and it is too critical to touch and too opaque to change. Modernizing it feels out of reach. The knowledge left with its makers, and the resources for such a transformation simply aren't in-house. Often nobody can even say whether it's possible at all.

The deliverable

Archiku recovers that knowledge, cost-effectively. A seasoned architect and analyst, backed by an agentic workflow, digs through your codebase and produces an accelerated validated baseline analysis in days: the functional essence as standard, extended where needed with a migration analysis or issue and bug detection.

The benefits

Every finding is traceable to the exact line of code, independently reviewed by multiple agents, with your experts in the loop as the final authority. Your team starts from a base that took days, not months.

Why Archiku

Agents excel at unstructured work: reading, researching, connecting the dots. The industry points them at generating output. Archiku points them the other way.

Archiku lets agents do what they do best. They read your entire codebase, trace behaviour to the source, map the business essence, and generate the input your people work from.

We call this software archaeology: digging through the layers of a system until the original intent surfaces again.
